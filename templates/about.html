TimeTable Pro is an automated academic scheduling system designed to simplify timetable generation for educational institutions of all sizes.

It efficiently manages departments, faculties, and courses — ensuring structured, organized academic planning with zero scheduling conflicts. Labs are assigned consecutive period pairs, theory sessions are spread evenly across the week, and no faculty member is ever double-booked.

The system supports odd and even semester separation, configurable break periods, customizable working days, and flexible start times — giving administrators full control over the academic calendar.
